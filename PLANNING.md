## Overview
Mether is a large-scale magical fantasy dimension mod featuring a vibrant crystal-based world. Players explore a bright, colorful dimension filled with magical crystals, mystical creatures, and powerful bosses. The mod includes extensive progression through 4+ tiers of equipment, 10+ crystal types, complex crafting systems, and 7+ unique biomes.

---

## Core Concept
- **Main Theme:** Magical Fantasy Crystal Dimension
- **Dimension Name:** Mether (The Crystal Realm)
- **Atmosphere:** Bright, colorful, welcoming yet challenging
- **Visual Style:** Glowing crystals, floating particles, magical effects
- **Color Scheme:** Multi-colored (rainbow crystals with unique properties)

---

## Dimension Details

### Access Method
- **Portal Type:** Crystal Altar structure
- **Portal Frame:** Built from Polished Luminite Stone (crafted from overworld materials)
- **Activation Item:** Crystal Key (crafted from diamonds, amethyst, and other rare materials)
- **Activation:** Place Crystal Key on altar pedestal to ignite portal

### Environment
- **Danger Level:** Mixed (peaceful crystal gardens and dangerous corrupted zones)
- **Sky:** Bright magical sky with floating crystal particles, aurora effects, shooting stars
- **Lighting:** Natural glow from crystals, no need for torches in many areas
- **Biomes:** 7+ unique crystal biomes
- **Structures:** Crystal geodes, floating islands, crystal temples, ancient towers, boss arenas

## Content Scale

### Crystal Types (10+ varieties)
1. **Luminite Crystal** (White/Silver) - Basic tier, glowing properties
2. **Rubicite Crystal** (Red) - Fire properties, heat resistance
3. **Sapphirite Crystal** (Blue) - Water/ice properties, swimming bonuses
4. **Emerite Crystal** (Green) - Nature properties, growth acceleration
5. **Citrite Crystal** (Yellow) - Lightning properties, speed bonuses
6. **Amethine Crystal** (Purple) - Magic properties, enchantment bonuses
7. **Obsidite Crystal** (Black) - Shadow properties, stealth bonuses
8. **Celestite Crystal** (Cyan) - Sky properties, flight/levitation
9. **Pyrope Crystal** (Orange) - Energy properties, power amplification
10. **Prismarite Crystal** (Rainbow) - All properties combined, top tier

### Blocks (30+ custom blocks)
- **Terrain Blocks:** Crystal Grass, Crystal Dirt, Crystal Stone (variants for each biome)
- **Ore Blocks:** Each crystal type has ore variant
- **Crystal Blocks:** Pure crystal blocks (decorative and storage)
- **Functional Blocks:** 
  - Crystal Altar (portal activation)
  - Crystal Forge (tier 1-2 crafting)
  - Advanced Crystal Synthesizer (tier 3-4 crafting)
  - Crystal Infuser (enchanting alternative)
  - Crystal Growth Chamber (crystal farming)
- **Decorative Blocks:** Crystal pillars, lanterns, glass, bricks
- **Plants:** Crystal flowers, glowing mushrooms, crystal vines

### Items (30+ custom items)
- **Crystal Shards:** Raw material from mining (10 types)
- **Refined Crystals:** Processed shards (10 types)
- **Crystal Dust:** Crafting component
- **Portal Items:** Crystal Key, Luminite Stone
- **Food Items:** Crystal Berries, Glowing Mushrooms, Crystal Honey
- **Special Items:**
  - Crystal Resonator (detects nearby crystals)
  - Crystal Compass (points to structures)
  - Growth Catalyst (speeds crystal growth)
  - Essence Vials (store crystal energy)
  - Teleportation Crystals (fast travel)

### Equipment Tiers

#### Tier 1: Luminite (Iron level)
- Basic crystal equipment
- Slight glow effect
- Standard durability

#### Tier 2: Elemental Crystals (Diamond level)
- Rubicite (Fire), Sapphirite (Ice), Emerite (Nature), Citrite (Lightning)
- Each has unique passive ability
- Higher durability
- Special visual effects

#### Tier 3: Advanced Crystals (Netherite level)
- Amethine (Magic), Obsidite (Shadow), Celestite (Sky), Pyrope (Energy)
- Powerful passive abilities
- Active abilities (right-click)
- Custom enchantments compatible
- Very high durability

#### Tier 4: Prismarite (Beyond Netherite)
- Ultimate tier combining all crystal properties
- Multiple passive abilities
- Multiple active abilities
- Highest durability
- Rainbow particle effects
- Requires boss materials to craft

#### Equipment Sets (Each tier includes)
- **Tools:** Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe
- **Weapons:** Sword, Crystal Staff (ranged magic), Crystal Bow
- **Armor:** Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots (each with set bonuses)

## Progression System

### Tier Progression
1. **Tier 1 (Luminite)** - Available immediately upon entering dimension
2. **Tier 2 (Elemental)** - Requires defeating Luminarch boss + exploring specific biomes
3. **Tier 3 (Advanced)** - Requires defeating 4+ elemental bosses + advanced crafting station
4. **Tier 4 (Prismarite)** - Requires defeating all 7 bosses + final boss core

### Boss Progression
- **Luminarch** - Required for tier 2 access
- **4 Elemental Bosses** - Mix of required (2) and optional (2) for tier 3
- **2 Advanced Bosses** - Optional but drop powerful items
- **Prismatic Overlord** - Required for tier 4 (final boss)

### Crafting Progression
1. **Crystal Altar** - Build in overworld to create portal
2. **Crystal Forge** - First crafting station in dimension (tier 1-2)
3. **Advanced Synthesizer** - Unlocked after first boss (tier 3)
4. **Prismatic Crafter** - Unlocked after defeating 4+ bosses (tier 4)

### Research System
- Collect crystal samples to unlock recipes
- Boss cores unlock advanced crafting
- Crystal fusion unlocks combination recipes

## Crafting System

### Complexity
- **Type:** Complex multi-station crafting system
- **Features:**
  - Multiple specialized crafting stations
  - Crystal fusion system
  - Multi-step processes
  - Energy-based crafting

### Crafting Stations
1. **Crystal Altar** - Portal activation (overworld)
2. **Crystal Forge** - Basic crystal tools and tier 1-2 equipment
3. **Crystal Infuser** - Add magical properties to items
4. **Advanced Crystal Synthesizer** - Tier 3 equipment and fusion
5. **Prismatic Crafter** - Ultimate tier 4 equipment
6. **Crystal Growth Chamber** - Farm and grow crystals
7. **Essence Extractor** - Extract energy from crystals

### Special Mechanics
- **Crystal Fusion:** Combine crystals for hybrid effects
- **Energy System:** Crystals store/transfer energy
- **Growth System:** Plant crystal shards to grow more
- **Resonance:** Crystals amplify each other when placed nearby
